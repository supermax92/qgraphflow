# Compact layout and connector clearance

`layout.version = templates-v4-clearance-compact-elkjs-0.11.0` records successful validation of the new rules. No additional business fields are required. Earlier layouts retain their geometry and version when opened, saved or exported. New clearance violations are advisory on those layouts; existing semantic, text and geometry errors still block output. Full arrange or automatic regeneration upgrades only an accepted result. A local edit applies new clearance checks to affected relations (including labels and changed boundaries), leaving unrelated legacy routes unchanged.

Routing checks visible outlines and text. Nonterminal clearance is at least 12 graph units (preferred 24); architecture overview cards use 6 (preferred 8). Only a relation's own first/last segment is exempt at its endpoint. Group borders may be crossed perpendicularly, but parallel lanes must stay at least 6 units away. Complete labels remain on their own paths. Sequence diagrams retain their message/activation layout.

Legal candidates minimize `area/nodeArea + length/(edgeCount*sqrt(nodeArea/nodeCount)) + 0.5*bends/edgeCount + 2*crossings/edgeCount`. Aspect and stable identifiers break ties. Crossings are a cost, not an absolute veto; necessary detours and unavoidable crossings remain supported. Explicit ranks, order, primary paths, type-specific partitions and ownership take priority. Refinement attempts subtree placement, peer ordering and empty-channel compression within 60 evaluations. It can retain and translate clear routes through a compressed channel before repairing conflicts.

The generator and offline Worker share geometry, scoring and quality checks. HTML, SVG and PNG use the saved routes and labels; exports never relayout. Diagnostics report per-edge minimum clearance, boundary-hugging length, bends, short segments and detour ratio; refinement reports area, total route length and the search termination reason. New safety violations block application. A bounded search failure does not prove that no valid layout exists. Cancellation or failure retains the last valid canvas and editable draft; view state remains isolated by `viewId`.
