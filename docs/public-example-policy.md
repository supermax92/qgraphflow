# Public example policy

Public source, documentation, examples, screenshots, demos, test fixtures and distribution packages must use independently authored fictional content or appropriately public source material.

Do not copy a private organization or project's names, repository paths, internal architecture, dependency rules, versions, implementation status, reference images or other identifying details. Removing a name alone does not make private-derived content a public example. Replace the content with an independent example and remove its old generated pages, images, recordings and packages.

Label fictional examples clearly. Keep public source-backed examples traceable to their public repository. Check both source files and packaged artifacts before delivery; do not reuse historical screenshots after replacing their input.
